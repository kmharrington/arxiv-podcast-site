- **Post-fabrication trimming of a 1024-pixel, single-photon counting, Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detector array with a pixel pitch of 150 micron** — Wilbert Ras-Vinke et al. — [arXiv:2609.10146](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10146v1) — A post-fabrication correction method for tightly packed MKID arrays cuts resonator frequency scatter from 1.1×10⁻² to 3.5×10⁻⁴ and raises yield from 76% to 94%.
- **Differential Polarization Calibration: A Consistency Test for Cosmic Birefringence** — Anto I. Lonappan, Brian Keating, Kam Arnold — [arXiv:2609.09149](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09149v1) — A birefringence-blind differential estimator reconstructs detector polarization-angle differences from eight Planck NPIPE detector-set maps and agrees with the Minami–Komatsu calibration pattern (χ² = 8.18 for 7 d.o.f., PTE = 0.32).
- **Optical Depths from the Thermal Sunyaev-Zel'dovich Effect with ACT DR6 and DESI DR1 Spectroscopic Galaxies and Optically-Selected Clusters** — J. E. Moore et al. — [arXiv:2609.08939](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08939v2) — Stacked tSZ measurements of DESI DR1 LRGs, BGS, and eROMaPPer clusters using ACT DR6+Planck maps, with detailed dust/CIB systematics treatment and new ȳ–τ̄ scaling relations consistent with pairwise kSZ optical depths.
- **Beyond the BLUE I: the advantage ceiling - how much can any estimator beat the matched filter in mm/submm survey data?** — Kaustuv Basu — [arXiv:2609.10475](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10475v1) — Defines an "advantage ceiling" η from Fisher information that bounds how far any estimator, including a neural network, can improve on the matched filter across a taxonomy of mm/submm survey noise.
- **What a Random Draw from the MCP Registry Contains, and What Tool-Use Benchmarks Contain Instead** — Haseeb Mohammed Afsar — [arXiv:2609.10962](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10962v1) — An unrepaired random sample of 400 servers from a 24,135-server MCP registry finds 48.8% complete an initialize handshake, and compares real tool descriptions against BFCL v4 and UltraTool near-duplication rates.
