- **Post-fabrication trimming of a 1024-pixel, single-photon counting, Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detector array with a pixel pitch of 150 micron** — Wilbert Ras-Vinke et al. — [arXiv:2609.10146](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10146v1) — Post-fabrication trimming of a tightly packed 1024-pixel MKID array cuts resonator frequency scatter from 1.1×10⁻² to 3.5×10⁻⁴ and raises multiplexed yield from 76% to 94%.
- **Differential Polarization Calibration: A Consistency Test for Cosmic Birefringence** — Anto I. Lonappan, Brian Keating, Kam Arnold — [arXiv:2609.09149](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09149v1) — A birefringence-blind differential estimator reconstructs Planck NPIPE detector polarization angles independently of the foreground-EB model and agrees with the Minami–Komatsu pattern (χ² = 8.18 for 7 dof, PTE = 0.32).
- **Optical Depths from the Thermal Sunyaev-Zel'dovich Effect with ACT DR6 and DESI DR1 Spectroscopic Galaxies and Optically-Selected Clusters** — J. E. Moore et al. — [arXiv:2609.08939](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08939v2) — High-significance stacked tSZ measurements on DESI DR1 LRGs, BGS, and eROMaPPer clusters yield Compton-y–to–optical-depth scaling relations consistent with pairwise kSZ, after a detailed dust/CIB/radio foreground study.
- **Beyond the BLUE I: the advantage ceiling - how much can any estimator beat the matched filter in mm/submm survey data?** — Kaustuv Basu — [arXiv:2609.10475](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10475v1) — Defines a computable Fisher-information ceiling η that bounds how much any estimator, neural or otherwise, can beat the matched filter, and shows a ResNet regressor falls well short of it.
- **What a Random Draw from the MCP Registry Contains, and What Tool-Use Benchmarks Contain Instead** — Haseeb Mohammed Afsar — [arXiv:2609.10962](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10962v1) — An unrepaired random sample of 400 MCP servers finds only 48.8% start at all, while popular tool-use benchmarks are dominated by near- and exact-duplicate rows that real MCP tools do not exhibit.
